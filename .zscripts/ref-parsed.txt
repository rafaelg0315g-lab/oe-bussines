 03 Method
 From an app to an audience. 
 01 
 Collect
 Partner apps send event-level signals through a lightweight integration — only for users who opted in under the app’s own privacy notice.
 02 
 Protect
 Direct identifiers are dropped or replaced, and free text is classified on the device. The topic leaves the app; the words never do.
